## Runtime requirement

WildQuest's goal is to be all-in-one and must not require Questie or QuestieDB to be loaded. Quest data included in WildQuest must be independently packaged and must not require these external addons at runtime. Until a distributable, provenance-reviewed data bundle is available, do not claim full quest database coverage or complete authored routes.

Route-ID coverage may inform original WildQuest-authored content. Do not copy source route scripts or prose verbatim.
